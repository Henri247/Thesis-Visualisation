Extraction + HPAL refining

Sulawesi, Indonesia.

The dissertation identifies the heaviest concentration of local environmental damage and the most precarious labour conditions in the Indonesian extraction and HPAL-refining stages.

DeforestationWater contaminationHazardous workLow wagesLong hoursLand conflictValue drain

Environmental expropriation

Mining/refining is connected to deforestation, water and soil contamination, land conflict and coal-intensive energy use.

Labour exploitation

Low wages, long hours, hazardous work and weak bargaining power are interpreted as mechanisms of high surplus extraction.

Accumulation

The analysis argues that undervalued ore/MHP and Chinese control over refining contribute to outward value drains.

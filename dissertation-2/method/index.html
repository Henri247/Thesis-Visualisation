CHAPTER 02 — METHOD

Follow the Thing.

Nickel is not treated as a single perfectly traceable shipment. It is used as a demonstrative lens through which different social, economic and ecological relations can be compared across EV production.

WHY THIS WORKS VISUALLY

The method is already a journey.

That makes the thesis naturally suited to an interactive map: the site's interface mirrors the dissertation's analytical sequence.

Choose nickel as a critical battery input that crosses multiple geographically distinct production nodes.
Use Volkswagen as the case through which to examine a German-led EV value chain.
Trace extraction/refining, chemical refining, battery-cell production, assembly and recycling.
At each node, collect background, environmental and labour evidence.
Interpret labour and accumulation using unequal-exchange frameworks.
Compare the full chain using ecological unequal exchange and Just Transition.

LIMITATION KEPT VISIBLE

The dissertation warns that data availability is uneven across nodes, especially for Chinese labour conditions. The site does not pretend otherwise.

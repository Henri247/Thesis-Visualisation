CHAPTER 09 — CONCLUSION

A just transition for whom?

The dissertation argues that European e-mobility can deliver ecological and labour benefits in core regions while reproducing 'unjust transitions' upstream.

ECONOMIC WINNERS

German lead firms + Chinese midstream refiners

The conclusion identifies these actors as key sites of value capture in the strand studied.

UPSTREAM COSTS

Workers, communities + environments in Indonesia

The conclusion contrasts downstream gains with low-paid dangerous labour, displacement and ecological damage upstream.

JUST TRANSITION

The central challenge is not only whether transport is decarbonised, but how the social and ecological costs of that decarbonisation are distributed internationally.

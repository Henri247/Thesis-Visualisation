CASE STUDY 02

Nike-linked manufacturing in Vietnam.

A manufacturing case examining wages, working time, gendered labour, union suppression and the bargaining asymmetry between lead firms and supplier factories.

EXTERNALISATION

Production is outsourced. Market power is not.

The dissertation examines supplier factories as part of a lead-firm system shaped by deadlines, cost competition and control over market access.

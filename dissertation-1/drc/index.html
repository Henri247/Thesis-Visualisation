CASE STUDY 01

Cobalt mining in the DRC.

A resource-extraction case tracing labour conditions, subcontracting, bargaining power and value-chain dependence in a globally strategic mineral sector.

SPATIAL STORY

Extraction → processing → global brands

The case follows cobalt from Southern Katanga into internationally controlled processing and battery supply chains.

● Mining region--- indicative chain route

EVIDENCE SNAPSHOT

What the case study surfaces

These values reproduce figures reported in the dissertation and are not a new dataset.

500k–1mpeople estimated to work in artisanal-scale cobalt mining
12–20%share of DRC cobalt attributed to artisanal-scale mining
63%of miners questioned in one USDoL report had been injured or made sick by work
40,000children estimated to be working in Southern DRC mines in the cited evidence
57%of LSM workers questioned by RAID worked through subcontracts

INTERPRETATION

The case links precarious and subcontracted labour to weak bargaining power, buyer dependence and the DRC's structurally subordinate position in higher-value global production.

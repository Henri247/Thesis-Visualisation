CHAPTER 01 — THEORY

Two ways to read the same supply chain.

The dissertation deliberately contrasts mainstream international-business explanations with a Marxist political-economy critique.

MAINSTREAM / IB

Trade as opportunity

  • Comparative advantage
  • FDI-driven development
  • Productivity and upgrading
  • GVC coordination and supplier development

The dissertation treats these frameworks as useful for explaining firm organisation and proposed development benefits, but limited in explaining structural labour exploitation.

CRITICAL / MARXIST

Trade as structured power

  • Unequal exchange
  • Surplus-value extraction
  • Colonial histories
  • Lead-firm / supplier asymmetry
  • Labour centred analysis

Critical frameworks are used to ask where value is created, where it is captured and why low wages and weak bargaining power persist.
